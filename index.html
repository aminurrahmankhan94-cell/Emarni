                    About Emarni

                    Connecting People. Building a Better Digital Future.

                    Who we are

                    Emarni is a messaging platform developed by Raflido Studios, a software company from Bangladesh dedicated to innovation and meaningful technological advancement.

                    The journey of Emarni began with a vision in 2024: to create a communication platform that brings people closer while promoting a safer, cleaner, and more respectful digital environment.

                    Our Mission

                    Our mission is to create a digital environment where people can communicate freely and confidently without exposure to harmful or inappropriate activities.

                    Emarni is designed with security and user protection at its core. Through intelligent moderation and advanced security measures, we aim to maintain a positive experience for our users.

                    Our Vision

                    We envision a future where communication is not limited by internet access or data costs. By 2035, our goal is to enable people around the world to communicate without requiring a conventional mobile data package.

                    Emarni Radio & TV

                    As part of our long-term vision, Raflido Studios plans to introduce Emarni Radio & TV, bringing satellite-based radio and television broadcasting to the Emarni platform — accessible without mobile data or an internet connection.

                    Developed by Raflido Studios

                    Emarni is proudly developed by Raflido Studios, a Bangladesh-based software company. All intellectual property rights and copyrights associated with Emarni are owned by Raflido Studios.

                    Our Commitment

                    At Emarni, we are committed to building more than a messaging application. We are building a platform that brings people closer, encourages positive communication, and opens new possibilities for a connected digital future.

                    Emarni — A New Era of Communication.

                      Before you continue

                      • Messages, posts, ads and adoption listings are visible to the people they are shared with. Posts, ads and pet listings are visible to every member.
                      • Messages and posts are not end-to-end encrypted.
                      • Emarni admins can review any conversation for safety and moderation. Every review is logged.
                      • A safety copy of a deleted message is kept for 30 days. Your password is never visible to anyone, including admins.
                      • Calls go directly between devices and are not recorded by Emarni.
