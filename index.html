            About Emarni

            Connecting People. Building a Better Digital Future.

            Who we are

            Emarni is a messaging platform developed by Raflido Studios, a software company from Bangladesh dedicated to innovation and meaningful technological advancement.

            The journey of Emarni began with a vision in 2024: to create a communication platform that brings people closer while promoting a safer, cleaner, and more respectful digital environment.

            At Emarni, we believe technology should serve humanity and contribute to a better world. Our mission is to make communication accessible, secure, and meaningful for everyone.

            Our Mission

            Our mission is to create a digital environment where people can communicate freely and confidently without exposure to harmful or inappropriate activities.

            Emarni is designed with security and user protection at its core. Through intelligent moderation bots and advanced security measures, we aim to prevent inappropriate activities and maintain a positive experience for our users.

            We are committed to continuously improving our platform to make it more secure, reliable, and accessible.

            Our Vision

            We envision a future where communication is not limited by internet access or data costs.

            By 2035, our goal is to enable people around the world to communicate without requiring a conventional mobile data package or internet subscription.

            We believe that access to communication should be a fundamental part of a connected world, and we are working toward making this vision a reality.

            Emarni Radio & TV

            As part of our long-term vision, Raflido Studios plans to introduce Emarni Radio & TV, bringing satellite-based radio and television broadcasting to the Emarni platform.

            Our goal is to make radio and television accessible to users without requiring mobile data or an internet connection.

            We also envision a platform where users can create and operate their own radio and television stations, allowing them to share their creativity, ideas, and content with audiences around the world.

            These initiatives represent our commitment to expanding the possibilities of digital communication and entertainment.

            Developed by Raflido Studios

            Emarni is proudly developed by Raflido Studios, a Bangladesh-based software company focused on building innovative digital products and technological solutions.

            All intellectual property rights and copyrights associated with Emarni are owned by Raflido Studios.

            Our Commitment

            At Emarni, we are committed to building more than just a messaging application.

            We are building a platform that brings people closer, encourages positive communication, and opens new possibilities for a connected digital future.

            Emarni — A New Era of Communication.

              • Messages, posts, ads and adoption listings are visible to the people they are shared with. Posts, ads and pet listings are visible to every member.

              • Calls go directly between devices and are not recorded by Emarni.
